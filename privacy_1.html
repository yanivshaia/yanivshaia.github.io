TaskPro Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

TaskPro ("the app") is a personal task and calendar application for Android, developed and operated by Yaniv Shaia ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

1. Data stored on your device

All tasks, projects, labels, routines, reminders, comments, settings and backups are stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. We do not operate any server and never receive this data.

2. Google account data

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests the following permissions, used solely for the purposes stated:

  • Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar) — to list your calendars so you can choose one, and to create, read, update and delete the calendar events that mirror your TaskPro tasks in that calendar, and to import events from that calendar as tasks when you enable two-way sync.
  • Google Drive app data (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata) — to save and restore your own TaskPro backup file in a hidden, app-specific folder of your Google Drive. The app cannot see or access any other file in your Drive.
  • Basic profile / email — to display which account is connected.

Google data is transferred directly between your device and Google's servers over HTTPS. It is not transmitted to, stored by, or shared with the developer or any third party. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Device calendars and microphone

With your permission the app reads and writes events in calendars already on your device (Android Calendar Provider) to display them next to your tasks, and uses the microphone only while you tap the voice-input button, through the device's speech recogniser. Audio is not recorded or stored by the app.

4. Notifications and alarms

Reminders use local notifications and alarms on your device only.

5. Analytics, advertising, tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and collects no usage data.

6. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. You can disconnect your Google account at any time in Settings → Google Calendar; revoking access at myaccount.google.com/permissions removes the app's access to Google data. Backups in your Drive app-data folder can be deleted from the Drive settings ("Manage apps").

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
