TaskPro

A personal task manager and calendar for Android — natural-language quick add, time-blocking timeline, Eisenhower matrix, routines, and two-way sync with Google Calendar.

What it does

  • Projects, sections, sub-tasks, labels, priorities, importance & urgency levels
  • Timeline and week planner with drag-and-drop time blocking; month, agenda and year views
  • Shows your phone's calendars alongside tasks; create and edit events
  • Two-way sync of tasks into a Google Calendar of your choice
  • Reminders, routines, weekly review, home-screen widget, voice capture
  • Local JSON backup and optional private backup to your Google Drive

Google account access

TaskPro asks for Google Calendar access only to create, update and read the events that mirror your tasks in the calendar you select, and for Google Drive app-data access only to store your own backup file in a private app folder. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the developer. See the privacy policy.
